Records policy

  1. Your records are yours. You can see what we hold and ask us to send it anywhere. We do not need a reason.
  2. Every release is confirmed by you, in person, each time. Nobody acting on your behalf can confirm one for you — not a family member, not an assistant, not us.
  3. One release, one recipient, one scope. A confirmation authorises exactly the recipient and the records named in front of you when you confirm it.
  4. Routine records travel together: visit summaries, labs, imaging, immunisations and prescriptions.
  5. Restricted records do not — at all, through an agent. Mental health, substance use and genetic records are never released through an agent-prepared disclosure. An assistant cannot include them, whatever it asks; a person who needs them must request them another way.

A demonstration, not a clinic. Every name, visit and document here is fictional. Built with 3way — evidence that the exchange model can preserve an explicit recipient and scope when the commitment is disclosure rather than money.
