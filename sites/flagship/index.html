One more thing. We only ever ask you to confirm a refund — never your assistant. Your device verifies it's you, and the server checks that signature before the refund moves. That isn't a bug to route around. It's the whole point.

On a browser that reports no fingerprint or face unlock — ChatGPT's in-app browser is one — this demo falls back to accepting an ordinary click, labelled in the transcript as exactly that: a note that somebody clicked, not proof of who.
